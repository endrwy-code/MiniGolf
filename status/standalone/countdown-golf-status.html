How many people get to play

Simulated with tools/flow_sim.py: a 3-hour session, a queue that never empties, groups of 4, hole times varying about ±35%, 300 runs per case. A model, not a measurement: time the real holes at the dry run.

≈ 2.3×as many if each group plays one 5-hole district when the queue is long: about 540–600 people
  • The slowest hole sets the pace. Four hero holes a minute slower than the rest cut capacity by about a quarter (77 → 56 an hour). Keep every hole within about 30 seconds of the others.
  • Free order doesn't help. It serves 5–15% fewer players, because groups lose time finding free holes.
  • Capping the course at about 12 groups costs little and cuts rounds by 10–25 minutes. The waiting moves to the queue.

Budget ceiling: not set yet. It's the biggest gap, and it's due at Gate 0 on Fri 20 Nov. It decides which of these rows we're on.
  • Why the main estimate is S$35–45k: it's priced like a small stage set. The raised deck drives the materials, and the four hero props are about a third of the labour (≈ 120 person-days in all).
  • The city look comes from cheap things: one rail colour, one sign design, painted skyline flats, tape "streets" and the room's lights. A carpenter-led or DIY build can still carry it.
  • Rented carnival lanes and the foam kit are plain and short. Use them as a fallback, extra kids' holes, or a price floor.
  • Don't cheapen the rails. Solid timber held still; foam, pool noodles and PVC pipe kill the rebound.

Safety flags raise now

  • Two circuits are over 100% on the cable plan: ND18 (LX4.2) at 101% and ND21 (LX6) at 106%. Breakers can trip mid-event. Raise it with whoever owns power, golf or not.
  • Trip hazards: cable runs cross both floor halves. Holes and queues must not add trip points. The walk from the hub to hole 5 runs along drop D42 for about 6 m and needs a continuous cable cover.
  • No slack yet: several plaza clearances sit exactly at the 1.2 m and 1.5 m limits.
  • Course power: 12/24 V only, from a dedicated RCD-protected circuit, never ND18 or ND21.
