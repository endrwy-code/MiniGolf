What changed on 27 Nov

Iteration 1: doc 14's holes on a factory deck
  • Play while you walk (28 Nov). Golf is now part of walking, not a separate mode. Click the view and the mouse looks around with a crosshair (no button to hold). You carry a putter and ball: walk to any tee (they glow), click to place the ball, walk up to it, aim with the crosshair, hold the mouse button and let go to putt. You can walk while it rolls, choose where to stand, and play the holes in any order. You walk on the decks at their real height, and the "behind you" camera no longer goes through walls.
  • Play it (new). Every hole is playable with a putter and ball: the ⛳ button, "Play this hole" on a hole's card, or F at a tee while walking. The ball rolls on the holes exactly as modelled (steps, ramps, banks, tubes, pegs) with doc 14's 0.6 m/s² carpet drag, and the speed bar shows m/s so you can try doc 14's paces. Special rules: one-shot curling on 9, the rally on 7, plinko slots on 1. A model to argue with, not a rig result. Shift now sprints while you walk.
  • Iteration-1 holes in every slot. All ten holes are now doc 14's New York iteration 1, redesigned after the team's Kulnari visit (doc 13). Tap a hole for how it plays, its mechanism, build, sizes and Kulnari lesson.
  • Layout v4. New positions and walk routes; every clearance check passes. Holes 9, 6 and 3 need a little more floor than doc 14's boxes, because doc 14's own numbers don't fit them: hole 9 is 5.13 m long, hole 6 is 4.55 m and hole 3 is 2.55 m wide.
  • Factory deck. For the 1688 factory route every hole is a raised deck. Its lowest playing surface sits 100 mm off the floor, and doc 14's heights are kept relative to that surface.
  • Holes 4 and 5 lose their raised greens. In the floor-laid route C they were there only to give the cup depth; the deck does that now. Gridlock's zebra crossing is printed flat and the Central Park lawn stays flat.
  • Rails. The white cap is exactly 100 mm above the surface it guards, and steps or slopes with it.
  • Shared scenery. The brownstone façade behind 10 and 9 (stoops, pizzeria shopfront, braced through the hole frames plus one L-jack), the subway kiosk on Gridlock's sidewalk, the park-wall cut-out between 5 and 4, festoons on 3 m poles (park and Times Square, at least 2.4 m up), charcoal "asphalt" Downtown and light-grey "sidewalk" Midtown, zebra crossings between holes, and the two hub portals: the Downtown bridge arch and the Midtown park gate, which now carry the district names. Each totem has its warm flood at 2.1 m. The portals are the first cut if money is tight.
  • Hole numbers as rounded squares. The totem badge, the hole buttons and the floating hole numbers are rounded squares, not round subway-style bullets (doc 13, section 5.8: a coloured circle with a number is too close to the licensed line bullets).
  • Cups. Flush 108 mm cups with a 64 mm sleeve and no raised collar (doc 13, section 5.3), so the renders can't be read as a flange.
  • Review fixes (to confirm). Liberty's banked corner starts with a 0.8 m tapered run-in along the outer rail instead of a vertical end face, and its closed back slopes down below the rail caps; the full-width rig on Sat 5 Dec should confirm it. Hot Dog Cart: the fries carton sits on the counter, out of the lane, and "Relish the Moment" is raised so it reads from the tee. Grand Central's clock has a face on both sides, so it also looks at the 2027 tower.
  • This model is the design reference. It shows what each hole looks like, how it plays and its true size, for the factory's reference renders. It is not the build guide: a separate professional build guide with dimensioned drawings and specs follows, made from the same numbers.

What changed on 20 Nov

A longer room and two delay screens
  • The room is longer. The team says the floor past the golf limit is about twice as deep as the 19 Nov photo estimate: about 20 m, so the back wall is about 47 m from the stage front (range 44–50 m). That matches the old cable plan (console about 20.7 m past the runway tip) and leaves about 470 m² for other activities after the console, not 220 m². Estimated, not measured: tape the golf limit to the back wall.
  • Two delay screens, not one. They hang parallel to the runway, one over each edge, about 2.4 m apart and back to back: one faces the 11.65 m side, the other the LF-platform side. Underside about 3.5 m (photos).
  • Exits in the 11.65 m wall. The photos show two exit doors on that side. Where they're guessed, the street-sign totems of holes 7 and 6 stand about 1.7 m in front of them. Confirm the doors, then move the totems if needed. The skyline flats leave gaps at the exits.
  • Runway width check. The performers on the runway only come out at a normal height if its white top is about 2.0 m wide. The model keeps the measured 2.4 m.

What's measured, what's assumed

Green dot = measured with a tape
  • Measured 18 Nov: width 25.6 m (11.65 + 2.4 runway + 10.25 + 1.3 LF platform), runway 22.54 × 2.4 m, golf limit 27 m from the stage front, speaker stands 0.515 m out from the runway.
  • Estimated, not measured: room length (back wall ≈ 47 m from the stage front: the team's sense of the room and the old cable plan, 20 Nov); from the 19 Nov photos: ceiling ≈ 8.5 m, side trusses ≈ 5.8 m, surround speakers ≈ 3.7 m, the two delay screens (underside ≈ 3.5 m), deck heights ≈ 0.9 m, side stairs with four risers, wall finishes, the stage screens and the side-wall exits.
  • Not measured, drawn where the sketch shows them: stage depth (6 m used; the photos hint it's deeper), stair and speaker-stand positions, the stage's curved corners, the sound console's size and position, the LF platform's length and the back-wall doors.
  • Design targets from doc 14, not measurements: factory decks with the lowest playing surface 100 mm up, rail caps 100 mm above the surface, flush 108 mm cups, 2 m street-sign totems, skyline flats 2.4–3.6 m, the 2.4 m brownstone façade, the 2.8 m ball-drop tower, the 2.6 m statue on its 0.6 m pedestal, festoon lights no lower than 2.4 m. Playing surfaces, rails, slopes and cups follow doc 14 to about ±10 mm in height and ±20 mm in plan; props are simplified shapes at true size, not construction drawings.
  • Where doc 14's own numbers don't add up (hole 9's length, hole 7's ramp length, hole 5's legs against its box, hole 8's ace speed, hole 3's headroom and others), the model keeps doc 14's numbers and the doubts go back to the team. Hole positions (layout v4) are about ±0.5 m.
  • Screen content is a placeholder in the style of the current stage graphics. Your body in walk mode uses typical sizes: average adult 1.66 m (between a typical Singapore man, about 1.72 m, and woman, about 1.60 m); child about 8 years old, 1.28 m.
  • Not shown: floor cables. They were drawn on the old cable-plan geometry and still need re-placing.
