### 8 Hot Dog Cart

**Plays.** Putt under the cart's arch between the ketchup and mustard bottles, up a short kerb and into an open giant bun whose curved faces steer the ball along a mustard squiggle to the cup near its closed end. Too soft rolls back off the kerb; too hard rebounds off the rounded end. The friendly ace hole and the food photo.

**Mechanism.** Lane 1.3 m, floor-laid. Arch 430 mm high, full width; counter 0.6 m deep. 1:5 kerb to +75 at 2.7 m (about 1.2 m/s at its foot with drag, S1). Bun halves 1.3 m long, 150 mm high, mouth 0.90 m narrowing to a 0.40 m channel (faces about 13°), closed end R 0.20 m, cup 0.30 m from the end. Halves fill to the rails (gap 0); inner faces 5 mm EVA, mouth noses PVC angle. Ace about 2.2–2.4 m/s. Junior tee 1.0 m past the cart.

**Build.** Bun boxes with a bent 3 mm hardboard skin, counter and arch flat. Carpenter 1.0 day (0.75). Volunteers about 1.5 days: golden bun, Ø180 bottles touching the rails, fries, umbrella on a post outside the rail with a sandbag, red LED.

**Miss, risks, blends.** Short rolls back; wide stays in the lane; under the counter is at most 0.3 m in. Risks: mouth corners (PVC angle, and S3); the cup must be dead flush; aces over about 40% move the cup 0.1 m off axis. The street corner; a "FERRY →" sign points on. **11:59:** "Open till 11:59".
