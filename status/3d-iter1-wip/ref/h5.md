### 5 Central Park Loop

**Plays.** Midtown's teaching hole. A park path zig-zags twice; each curved outer rail carries a well-paced ball round, under a stone arch and up a little lawn to the cup in a fountain. Sign line: "the rail is your friend".

**Mechanism.** Path 0.9 m; legs 1.8, 1.2 and 2.2 m. Outer corners curved R 0.9 m, inner corners rounded and filled. Last 1.2 m a +75 green with a 1:5 bevel; cup 0.5 m past the crest. The design assumed 10–15% speed loss per corner (ace about 2.9–3.0 m/s); the review expects 25–30%, which makes an ace doubtful (S6). Junior tee at leg 2.

**Build.** Corners of five stacked 18 mm ply arcs, the top one forming the cap, screwed to plates every 300 mm: no spring-back, no free ends (K03's failure). EVA face in the design; the review prefers a hard face (S6). Carpenter 1.0 day (0.75). Volunteers about 1.5 days: arcs, two borrowed Christmas trees with fairy lights, the existing bike wheels outside the rails, benches, lamp posts, arch, fountain, blue LED.

**Miss, risks, blends.** A soft ball stops within 0.45 m of a rail; a hard one rides the curve. Risks: scrub (measure on rig (e); move the bevel if the loss exceeds 20%); tree bases breaking clearances (S6). Carries the old Bike Lane's wheels and pun. **11:59:** a park clock.
