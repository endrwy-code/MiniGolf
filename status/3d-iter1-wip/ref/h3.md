### 3 The Subway (the prototype)

**Plays.** Putt up the station steps. Funnel rails steer the ball into one of three tiled portals (left Express, centre Local, right Shuttle). It vanishes, races round a hairpin under a clear lid and shoots out on the opposite side heading back toward you, with the Express exit on the cup line. Walk round and putt out.

**Mechanism, redrawn (M1, M2).** A single ply wedge could only fall across the loop box, so every hairpin had zero pull at its entry and exit and a dead-slow ball would stall; a flat 0.8 m platform also stopped just-cresting balls and sent nose bounces back down the ramp. Now:

- **Platform** 0.5 m, falling 1:12 toward the portals: any ball that crests reaches a portal, and a nose bounce returns into a channel.
- **Portals** three × 110 mm, 30 mm PVC-faced round noses, funnel 1.0 → 0.42 m.
- **Lead-ins** 1:4 at each threshold, dropping at least 25 mm in the first 100 mm (about 37 and 50 mm for the middle and inner portals, because the loop floor slopes), so every loop starts at 0.3 m/s or more.
- **Loop floor** a plane falling across the box at 5–6°; hairpin radii 0.30, 0.44, 0.58 m; walls of three stacked 18 mm arcs; 4 mm polycarbonate lid, 60 mm headroom, 4 thumbscrews. Slope along each path averages about 3–4°, peaks at the apex and is zero at the ends, which is why the lead-ins exist.
- **Exits** step down to the return green (1.0 × 2.6 m at +67, cup 1.2 m out on the Express line) on carpeted 1:4 bevels, with no raw steps.

**Height check.** The review said "+190 if needed", but its drops don't fit under +190:

| Point | Height (mm) |
|---|---|
| Ramp crest | about +245 |
| Portal sills (0.5 m at 1:12) | about +203 |
| Outer loop entry (25 mm lead-in) | about +178 |
| Outer exit (5° across 1.16 m) | about +77 |
| Return green (10 mm carpeted step) | +67 |

So the target crest is **about +245 mm**: a 1:6 ramp about 1.47 m long, with the flat cut from 1.0 to 0.8 m to keep the U at 4.0 m. With slope drag that is about 2.3 m/s at the ramp foot and about 2.5 m/s from the adult tee, like hole 1. That breaks the review's own "at most about 1.8 m/s for a 6-year-old" (S1), so the junior tee at the ramp foot plus "after two roll-backs, place it on the platform for +1" carries small kids. If the ramp proves too hard, shorten the falling platform first; keep the cross-fall at 5° or more.

**Build.** Ramp and platform, loop box (about 1.3 × 0.75 m) with a plane floor and shaped lead-ins, arcs (carpenter cuts the master, CNC or volunteers the rest), lid, return green, a 0.4 m island box. The existing tile-wall sheet is re-cut with 3 portals. Carpenter 2.25 days (1.5). Volunteers about 1.5 days: white tile, invented line symbols (star, ring, diamond), a 1.6 m kiosk with battery globe lamps, ice-blue LED.

**Miss, risks, blends.** Too soft rolls back down the ramp; every channel falls; the lid lifts off in under 20 s. Risks: loop stall is still the course's main physics unknown (the lever is a bigger entry drop, not a bigger radius); polycarbonate fire rating; the gap script needs a lidded-channel exemption. Hole 2's queue watches the exits. **11:59:** the platform clock.
