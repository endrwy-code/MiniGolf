### 9 Brooklyn Pizza

**Plays.** One shot each, curling style: putt down the lane, up the "crust" kerb and onto a 1.6 m pizza. The ball's centre scores: centre pepperoni cup 1, inner circle 2, on the pizza 3, anywhere else 4. Balls stay until all four have putted, so knock-ons count. The youngest player calls close ones.

**Mechanism.** Lane 2.6 m, 0.95 m wide, flaring to 1.75 m. Crust: a full-width 1:5 bevel rising 75 mm, needing about 1.2 m/s at its foot with drag (M6; the design said 1.0–1.1), so dead-weight balls roll back and score 4. House: a +75 deck levelled to ±2 mm with the pizza, eight Ø150 pepperoni, the centre one as the flush cup, and a Ø0.60 m dashed inner circle. Centre 3.8 m from the tee; ace about 2.3 m/s; the 2-point window is about ±4% of speed.

**The oven (M6).** As designed, a smash bounced off the foam backstop back onto the pizza for a 3, so hitting hard paid. The run-out and foam are replaced by a full-width **oven gutter** behind the crust ring: 120 mm wide, 45 mm deep, its floor falling 3° to a 250 mm open pocket at one rail for hand pick-up. Any ball in the oven scores 4 and can't come back.

**Build.** Deck (2 sheets), bevel, flare walls, gutter and pocket. Carpenter 0.75 day (0.6). Volunteers about 1.5 days: the pizza (inlay, or painted per S12), a 1.2 m slice sign, the shopfront, "1 SHOT · CURLING" on the totem, orange LED.

**Miss, risks, blends.** Short rolls back; long drops into the oven; nothing to clear. Risks: needle-punch speed (strip on 3 Dec), seams (S12), floor flatness, kids asking for another go. The brownstone's ground floor. **11:59:** the menu board.
