# Review: visual check of the ten holes (iteration 1)

Reviewer agent, 27 Nov 2026. Review only. 14 image reads in total.

## How the images were made

- Pages: `assemble.py --layout layout-test.json` (all ten builders, on `base.html`), plus a copy of the test layout with every hole `mirror: true`. Both in the reviewer's scratchpad, not in `W/out`.
- One 2 × 2 sheet per hole, `--w 900 --h 560`: `cam=close`, `top`, `side` with `iso=1&light=house`, and `cam=tee&iso=1&light=glow`. Then three extra sheets: zoomed side elevations of 9, 8, 7, 1 (`zoom=2.2–2.4`); the mirrored page's 10 side and tee, 6 side and 3 side; and close-ups of 6, 8, 1 and hole 7's tee in house light. One sheet of the mirrored page: tops of 6, 5, 3 and hole 3's tee view.
- Tool problem: with this container's Chromium, `shot.py`'s `--headless=new --screenshot` returns blank tiles after about 3 s (the virtual-time budget is ignored). `chrome-headless-shell` from `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium_headless_shell-1243/` works (about 10 s per tile). `dump.py` works with `--headless=new`.

## Across all holes

- **Text**: no mirrored or back-to-front text anywhere, including the mirrored page (3's wall labels, 6's sign and statue, 5's signs, 10's sign and window).
- **Slopes**: every slope seen (9 crust, 8 kerb, 7 hill, 6 shore, 3 ramp and platform, 1 ramp and crest, 10 terraces) is smooth geometry, not stepped boxes, and every rail follows its steps and slopes with the cap 100 mm above the surface.
- **Should-fix (tool)**: `iso=1` doesn't hide the test layout's skyline flats and scenery (brown and navy flats fill the close and side views of 2, 9 and others). Add `layers.flats = false` to the hook's `iso` branch, or hide those objects. Also hide the selection label (`selLabel`) under `ui=0`: it sits over every render and hides hole 7's hung sign.
- **Totem**: the test pages use `base.html`, whose totem still has the round numbered bullet. `base-v2.html` already has the rounded square: check it in the final renders.
- **Glow lighting** tee views are dark but every sign and the LED glow read.

## Counts (visual)

Must-fix: 1 (hole 6 cheek, shared with fidelity MF1). Should-fix: 4 (hole 8 sign hidden from the tee; hole 6 corner block above the rails, shared with fidelity; tool: flats in `iso`; tool: selection label). Cosmetic: 7.
