# Request-for-quote templates

Copy, fill in the [brackets] and send. Attach `docs/03-hole-spec-sheet.md` (exported as a PDF) and `floorplan/layout-draft-zone-a.png` where it helps.

Don't share anything beyond what a supplier needs: no member details, no internal floorplan screenshots, and no power or network details. The simplified layout is enough.

The questions are designed to **test** suppliers as well as get prices. A supplier who answers vaguely, or won't give dates in writing, has told you something.

---

## A. Local set builder or exhibition fabricator (main route)

> Subject: Quote request: 10-hole indoor mini golf course, one-off event, install 28–30 Dec
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> We're [Church/Team] in Singapore and are planning a 10-hole indoor mini golf course for a one-off event on 31 Dec 2026, set up inside an auditorium. We'd like a quote to build it.
>
> **Scope** (draft spec attached):
> - 10 modular holes on raised plywood platforms (about 120 mm high), each made of panels two people can carry, bolted together, on rubber feet (nothing fixed to the venue floor)
> - Timber or plywood rails (100 mm), with a cap overhang hiding LED strip (low voltage)
> - Turf fitting (we can supply the turf, or you can; please price both)
> - 4 hero props: windmill, barn tunnel, giant clock green, and a "2027" arch
> - 4 mid-complexity features (pin field, tunnel wall, ramp, island bridge) and 2 simple lanes
> - Printed hole totems (10)
> - Delivery, install on 28–30 Dec, and removal on [date]
>
> **Please tell us:**
> 1. Price, broken down by hole and by item (platforms, props, LEDs, graphics, transport, install, removal).
> 2. Can you build a **prototype hole by 4 Dec** and have **all 10 finished for a dry-run assembly by 18 Dec**?
> 3. Your crew size and other December commitments.
> 4. Photos of 2–3 similar builds you've done (events, exhibitions, props), and one client we can speak to.
> 5. Fire-rating certificates you can provide for paints, vinyl and any foam.
> 6. A price for storage after the event, and what happens to the materials if we don't keep them.
> 7. Payment terms.
>
> We're deciding by **Wed 25 Nov**.
>
> Thanks,
> [Name], [Role]

## D. Factory (e.g. Huadong Play): test questions

Send this even if you're leaning local. Their answers tell you whether they're realistic for this date, and whether they're worth considering for a reusable course in 2027.

> Subject: Indoor mini golf: 10 holes, delivery to Singapore by 21 Dec 2026
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> We're considering an indoor mini golf course of 10 holes for delivery to Singapore.
>
> 1. Do you have **ready-made stock** of any holes that can ship this month? Please send photos of the **actual stock items**, not catalogue renders.
> 2. For a standard 10-hole package: price per hole (FOB and CIF Singapore), what's included (base, turf, borders, obstacles, LED, cups, putters and balls), and the **number of production days**.
> 3. Exact earliest **ship date** and **arrival date in Singapore**, in writing, if we pay the deposit on [date].
> 4. Packed volume (m³) and weight, so we can check freight costs and our load-in route.
> 5. Materials, with **flame-retardant certificates** (turf, fibreglass, wraps) and CE/TÜV or other test reports.
> 6. Installation: do you send a technician, or provide drawings and video guidance? Can modules be installed in 1–2 days by a local crew?
> 7. Three projects you've delivered outside China (preferably Southeast Asia), with the venue name and city so we can check them.
> 8. Payment terms, and what happens if delivery is late.
> 9. Your business licence name and factory address (for a video call or a factory tour).
>
> Thanks,
> [Name]

**How to read the answers**
- No ready stock and 20+ production days: **not viable for 31 Dec.** File them for a 2027 decision.
- Catalogue renders only, with no real photos or contactable references: treat as a trading company and be careful.
- They won't commit to dates in writing: walk away.
- Clear dates, real stock photos, references and certificates: worth a video call.

## E. Freelance carpenter (route C, about 12 days)

For route C in `11-route-c-carpenter-build.md`: one paid carpenter builds the structure, volunteers paint and dress. Attach the carpenter's pack (`carpenter-pack/countdown-golf-carpenter-pack.pdf`; how to use it: `12-carpenter-pack.md`).

> Subject: Carpentry for a one-off indoor mini golf course, about 12 days in December
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> We're [Church/Team] in Singapore, building a 10-hole indoor mini golf course for one night, 31 Dec 2026, in an auditorium. Volunteers will paint and dress everything. We're looking for a carpenter to build the structure.
>
> **The work** (drawings attached):
> - About 140 m of timber rail (45 × 95 mm on edge), in sections two people can carry, held down by plates under the playing surface. Nothing is fixed to the venue floor.
> - Ten low raised greens (about 60 mm, with a bevel) where the cups go, and a few simple features: a hump, a gangway and a wall with three tunnels.
> - Flat cut-out props on braced bases (1.5–3 m tall), a ball-collecting box and a small booth frame.
> - One prototype hole by Fri 4 Dec, the rest by Fri 18 Dec for a dry-run assembly, and help with the install on 28–29 Dec.
>
> **Please tell us:**
> 1. Your day rate, and how many days you'd estimate.
> 2. Which days you're free between 30 Nov and 29 Dec.
> 3. Whether you bring your own tools, and what you'd need from us (workspace, power, a helper).
> 4. Photos of 2–3 things you've built (sets, booths, furniture).
> 5. Whether you'd buy the timber or we should, and a timber merchant you'd recommend.
>
> We're deciding by **Wed 25 Nov**.
>
> Thanks,
> [Name], [Role]

**How to read the answers**
- Over about S$300 a day, or more than 15 days: route C won't fit S$10k without cuts. Check the cut list in `11-route-c-carpenter-build.md` section 6.
- Can't give fixed days in writing: keep looking. The route hangs on this one person.
