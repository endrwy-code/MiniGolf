## Design reasoning (28 Nov)

- Cup depth (doc 13: 60–68 mm, soft bottom) is the root constraint. Floor-laid holes need a raised box at every cup anyway (route C). A full low deck gives sunk cups anywhere, and terraces/slopes by rib height.
- Deck module built as a ply box (top on ply ribs ripped from the same sheet) instead of doc 03's 45 x 45 battens: rib material ≈ 0.25–0.4 sheet per module, so a deck costs about the same as floor-laid-with-a-full-base plus laminated rails, and much less than battens.
- One sheet, one module: rip 1220 into 900 (deck top) + 320 offcut → 5 ribs of 50 mm. Lane 900 mm wide inside the rails (rails as fascia outside the deck).
- Heights: pads 3 + ribs 50 + top 12 + carpet 4 = 75 mm surface (doc 03's 75 mm). Cup: 110 mm uPVC sleeve standing on the floor, 10 mm EVA disc → about 62 mm deep.
- Rail = fascia: 12 mm ply strip 172 mm tall glued + screwed to the doubled edge rib, stands ≈ 100 mm above the carpet; 60 mm cap on top, flush inside, overhang outside.
- Rail per hole ≈ 8–11 m; course ≈ 100 m full rails + 9 m low tee rails. Strips: 6 x 172 + 2 x 60 caps per sheet.
- Sheet count ≈ 27 decks/ribs/boosters + 8.5 rails/caps + 3 features + 2 frames ≈ 40 x 12 mm ≈ S$1,440 at S$36. Plus about 250 rip cuts.
- Cost check at S$3k: about S$3.7k with putters bought and 10% contingency → needs borrowing putters, fewer printed items, shorter holes. Verdict: tight but possible; S$4k comfortable.
- Rolling resistance: 0.6 m/s² carpet drag means a ball only starts rolling by itself on slopes above about 5° ((5/7) g sinθ > 0.6). So "drain-back" harbours need ~5°; dropped. Cross-falls of 1–2° only bend a moving ball.
- Ramp threshold, H = 60 mm at 1:5 (L 0.3 m): v² = 10gH/7 + 2aL = 0.84 + 0.36 = 1.20 → 1.10 m/s at the foot.
- Hill (hole 7) plateau 620 mm, cup at 310: reach cup needs v_crest ≥ 0.61 (v_foot ≥ 1.25); off-line ball runs over above v_crest 0.86 (v_foot 1.39).
