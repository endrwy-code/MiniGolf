Overall that's about 83 m² of playing surface and about 140 m of border (from the carpenter's pack). Each lane in pieces no longer than about 2.4 m, so two people can carry them and they fit through doors. Send the hole outlines from `carpenter-pack/countdown-golf-carpenter-pack.pdf` (or a DXF) with the request.

What to get in writing before paying:

1. **Real photos or a video** of the exact product (not renders), and a sample or swatch by courier if time allows.
2. **Dates**: ex-factory date and delivery to Singapore (DAP, by air) **no later than 10 Dec**, with a refund if it's late.
3. **Fire rating** of the turf and backing, with the test report: the venue may ask.
4. An itemised quote: lanes, borders, cups, flags, packing, air freight to Singapore.
5. Company name on the business licence = the contract = the bank account holder. Pay through **Alibaba Trade Assurance** (or 1688's checkout for a 1688 seller).

Singapore needs an import permit before the goods arrive, and GST (9%) is payable on import, so use a forwarder or ask for DAP with the paperwork handled (`04-sourcing.md` section 4).

## Finding the factory behind a Taobao or Pinduoduo shop (do it on a phone)

**Search terms** (paste into Taobao, Pinduoduo or the 1688 app):

| Chinese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 迷你高尔夫球道 | mini golf lane |
| 移动迷你高尔夫 / 便携式迷你高尔夫 | mobile / portable mini golf |
| 高尔夫推杆果岭 定制 | putting green, made to order |
| 模块化 推杆果岭 拼接 | modular putting green, joins together |
| 室内迷你高尔夫 球场 定制 | indoor mini golf course, made to order |
| 迷你高尔夫 障碍 道具 | mini golf obstacles and props |
| 果岭草 人造草 定制尺寸 | putting turf, cut to size |
| 夜光 / 荧光 迷你高尔夫 | glow / UV mini golf |

**Then, for each promising shop:**

1. Look for 工厂店 or 源头工厂 (factory shop), a shop that's several years old and has real buyer photos (买家秀) of the product at full size.
2. Open the shop's licence: on Taobao, the shop page → 店铺资质 / 营业执照; on Pinduoduo, the shop page → 店铺资质. Note the company name (公司名称).
3. Search that company name on the **1688** app (the wholesale side of Taobao): many Taobao factory shops sell there too, with a 定制 (custom) option and a phone number on the company page (联系方式).
4. Check it on 企查查, 天眼查 or 爱企查: registered address, phone, founding date and whether it's a manufacturer (生产) or a trader (贸易). Prefer manufacturers in Guangdong (Shenzhen, Foshan, Huizhou, Dongguan), where most of these products come from.
5. Message the shop's customer service (客服): 你们是工厂吗？可以按我们的尺寸定做吗？可以加微信吗？ ("Are you the factory? Can you make it to our sizes? Can we add you on WeChat?"), then send the Chinese message above.

**Small stock items** (cups, flags, putters, balls, practice mats) can be bought straight from Taobao and shipped through Taobao's own consolidated shipping to Singapore (官方集运). Check the current transit time in the app before relying on it for anything on the critical path.
