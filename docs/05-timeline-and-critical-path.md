## Why imported items struggle to fit

**Turf.** One verified factory quotes about 20 days of production for small orders (see `project-brief.md`). Ordered on 23 Nov, that means production finishes about 13 Dec, then air freight and clearance add about 3–7 days, so it arrives around **16–20 Dec**. That's after the build needs it. **Prefer turf that's already in stock in Singapore**, or order the imported turf **this week** with air freight confirmed in writing.

**A full factory-made course.** See Truth 3 in `01-how-to-think-about-this.md`:

```
Nov 16 ──── 23 ──── 30 ──── Dec 7 ──── 14 ──── 21 ──── 28 ─ 31
 [quote+design]
         [deposit]
           [============ production 15–30+ days ============]
                                        [== sea 7–14 d ==]
                                                     [install?]
 slack: none, during the pre-Christmas freight peak and the pre-CNY factory rush
```

This is only viable if a supplier has **ready-made stock** that ships within days, confirmed in writing with dates and a penalty or refund clause.

## Critical path (what can't slip)

1. Budget and install-window answers (G0)
2. Builder booked (G1)
3. Turf in hand (by about 7 Dec)
4. Prototype lessons applied (G2)
5. Dry-run assembly (G3)
6. Install (G5)

Hero props, graphics and the ops kit run alongside and can absorb small delays. **The turf and the builder's capacity are the two things most likely to sink the project.** Book the builder first, because December is the peak season for event fabricators.
