# Singapore benchmark visits: where to play before we build

Researched 22 Nov 2026 by Claude from web listings and reviews. Nobody on the team has visited yet. Prices and hours change, so check the venue's own page before going. Where a source is old, its date is given.

## Bottom line

Only three real mini golf courses are open in Singapore as of November 2026: **Kulnari Mystery Golf** (indoor), **Dino Putt** (outdoor) and **UltraGolf** (outdoor). Two 2026 guides agree on this ([Terris, Aug 2026](https://terris.sg/blog/best-mini-golf-singapore); [Sassy Mama, Nov 2026](https://www.sassymamasg.com/play-mini-golf-singapore/)). Most other "mini golf" listings are golf simulators.

For four people, Kulnari plus Dino Putt costs roughly S$300. That is an estimate from listed prices, not a quote.

## 1. Kulnari Mystery Golf (must go)

**What it is.** 18 holes, indoor and air-conditioned, in a 1920s shophouse on Circular Road. It is mini golf crossed with an escape room: players are detectives, each hole is a chapter of a murder mystery, and clues are hidden among the props. Dim lights, eerie sound effects, vintage furniture, "film set" styling. Hole 9 is a bar. ([TheSmartLocal, Jun 2023](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/kulnari-mystery-golf/); [Little Day Out, Oct 2022](https://www.littledayout.com/kulnari-mystery-golf-swing-back-1920s-mini-golf/); [Fever listing, current](https://feverup.com/m/427476))

**Rules that matter for our ops.** 6 shots per hole (we plan 5). Groups of at most 5, sent off in slots 5 minutes apart. A round takes 1 to 2 hours, which is about 3 to 7 minutes per hole: much slower than the 2 to 2.5 minutes our throughput plan assumes. Over 5s only. Not wheelchair accessible. (Fever)

**Price and booking.** Adult S$39, child (5–12) S$31, family ticket S$32 each, weekend group (4+) S$35.10. Book online through Fever. Hours in 2023 were roughly noon to 10 pm, longer at weekends: check current hours. Ratings: Google 4.8 from about 2,500 reviews; Fever 4.7.

**What to study.**
- **Light level.** This is the main question we have for production. How dim is it, and can you still see the ball, the cup and the floor edges? Take a rough reading with a free phone light-meter app at three or four holes, and note which holes felt too dark.
- **How the story slows play.** Do clues and puzzles hold groups up? Our "find the 11:59" must be something families do while they wait, not something that stops play. Time a few holes.
- **The spinning platform hole.** Compare it with our rink turntable (hole 3): how it is built, how it is guarded, whether it jams.
- **Set-dressing methods.** How much is real furniture, and how much is painted flats, prints and props? What reads well in dim light?
- **Borders, cups and transitions.** Rail height and material, how turf meets the border, how they stop a ball leaving the hole, steps between holes.
- **Hole 9 bar.** It works as a mid-course hub. Compare it with our Tee Booth at the runway tip.
- **Wear.** After four years open, what is scuffed, broken or patched? That shows which parts take the most punishment.

## 2. Dino Putt, clubFACE Jurong

**What it is.** 18 outdoor holes with no shelter, opened March 2025, among life-size animatronic dinosaurs that move and roar (T-rex, Stegosaurus, a pterosaur). Smaller dinosaurs sit around the course. Each dinosaur has an information board. Holes get harder as you go, with bends, humps and bunkers. Small steps lead between sections, and a pathway carries the dinosaur rides through the course. Turf is new and soft. Staff hand out a putter, ball, sunscreen and mosquito repellent. ([TheSmartLocal, Aug 2025](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/dino-putt-jurong/); [Little Day Out, Mar 2025](https://www.littledayout.com/dino-putt-dinosaur-mini-golf-course/))

**Time and price.** About 1 hour for 18 holes; one family of six took 1.5 hours. Adults S$35 weekday or S$40 weekend; children 12 and under S$30 or S$35; family pass (2 adults and 1 child) S$90 or S$105 (Aug 2025 prices). Walk-ins are accepted; book ahead for groups at weekends. Open daily 9:30 am to 10 pm. Go in the evening: it is cooler and you see the course under lights.

**What to study.**
- **Big props as obstacles.** Which dinosaurs change the shot, and which are only scenery? That is the line between a hero hole and a photo spot. Compare with our hero props: the Big Apple (8), Liberty (6), Grand Central (2) and the ball-drop tower (1).
- **Difficulty curve.** How do they make the later holes harder without slowing everyone down?
- **Hole signs.** The information boards are like our pun signs. Can people read them, and do they?
- **Turf and joins.** Look at how the turf is laid and how it meets the borders.

## 3. UltraGolf, The Palawan, Sentosa (optional)

**What it is.** 18 outdoor holes on about 1,900 m² of Palawan Beach, laid out like a real golf course: contoured greens you have to read, 11 bunkers, water hazards and no novelty obstacles. Every hole has two tees, one for beginners and one for experienced players. ([TheSmartLocal, Nov 2023](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/ultragolf-sentosa/); [Little Day Out, Jul 2023](https://www.littledayout.com/ultragolf-mini-golf-palawan-beach-sentosa/))

**Price and hours.** Adults about S$22–23, children S$18; off-peak weekday lunchtimes are cheaper. Weather insurance costs S$2. Open weekdays 12:30 to 9 pm and weekends from 10 am. Google 4.5.

**What to study.**
- **Two tees per hole.** This is a cheap idea we could borrow: a kids' tee and an adults' tee on each hole, for mixed-age groups. It costs almost nothing.
- **Breaks in the green.** Gentle slopes make plain holes interesting. Can our 75 mm decks carry a subtle break?
- **What "no decoration" feels like.** One reviewer called it "just average" and wanted more decoration. Notice whether you get bored by hole 10. Ten plain holes would not suit a party.

## 4. Universal Studios Singapore: New York zone (for the look, not golf)

A post-modern New York street with replica landmarks, neon signs, street facades and sidewalks. It is not mini golf, but it is the nearest built New York set in Singapore. ([Resorts World Sentosa](https://www.rwsentosa.com/en/play/universal-studios-singapore/zones/new-york)) Only worth it if someone on the team is already going. Look for how flat facades are made to read as 3D, how the neon signs are built and mounted, and which street furniture (hydrants, signs, subway details) sells "New York" at a glance. The zone is themed on films, so don't copy anything with a logo or a character.

## Not worth the trip

- **PLAYON, Downtown East** (opened 31 Oct 2026): three indoor putting strips with a hole and flag, aimed at children, included in the S$29–35 park pass. No obstacles. Useful only as a picture of what plain looks like. ([PLAYON press release, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/954731146/playon-adds-mini-golf-putting-lanes-at-downtown-east-giving-singapore-families-a-new-game-to-play-together))
- **Golf simulators listed as "mini golf":** The Par Club, Five Iron Golf, GolfX, Partee Golf, Hidden Castle Golf Club, Golfwav, Bogeymen. These are screen bays for full-swing golf, not putting courses. One list ([bestinsingapore, last updated Nov 2024](https://www.bestinsingapore.co/best-mini-golf-singapore/)) describes several of them as themed mini golf, so treat that list with care.
- **Closed:** Holey Moley, Clarke Quay (27 neon pop-culture holes, closed September 2022); LilliPutt (indoor, closed); Champions Golf, Bukit Timah (closed December 2023); Pixar Putt (a pop-up at Marina Bay Sands in August and September 2022, not back since).

## Watch for: Christmas mall pop-ups

Last Christmas (2025), some malls ran temporary mini golf courses: a LINE FRIENDS course at Frasers Property malls and a Sanrio course at Far East malls (including West Coast Plaza, 12–14 Dec 2025). No 2026 course had been announced when we checked on 22 Nov. If one opens in December, go and see it. It would be the closest thing to our build: temporary, indoor, laid on a finished floor and taken down after a few days. Look at how the bases are made, how borders join, how cables and edges are handled, and how they cope with crowds.

## Visit scorecard

Take this on every visit. One person keeps time and one takes photos.

| Check | How | Why it matters for us |
|---|---|---|
| Minutes per hole | Stopwatch from first putt to the last ball out, at 4–5 holes | Our capacity plan assumes 2–2.5 min for a group of 4 |
| Waiting at holes | Count the groups queued at each hole | Shows where play bunches up |
| Light level | Phone light-meter app, at the green and at the floor between holes | Gives production a real number for the "dim warm wash" |
| Can you see the ball? | Note any hole where you lost sight of the ball or tripped | Lighting and trip edges |
| Stuck balls | Note where a ball stopped out of reach and how staff got it back | Our "no unreachable spot" rule |
| Signs | Can you read the hole sign from the tee? | Our pun signs and hole numbers |
| What people photograph | Which holes got phones out | Tells us which hero props earn their cost |
| Best and worst hole | Each person names one of each, and says why | Our hole list for G1 |
| Wear and repairs | Scuffs, tape, broken props, patched turf | Which parts need to be strongest |
| Borders and cups | Height, material, joins; photograph them close up | The carpenter's details D1–D9 |

**Photos.** Ask staff before photographing set pieces, and keep other guests, especially children, out of the frame. Save the photos to `reference/benchmark-visits/` with the venue name and date in the file name.
