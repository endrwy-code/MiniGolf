# Renting: what's possible, how, and how good it is

Researched 18 Nov 2026. This session couldn't open the vendor websites directly (blocked by the session's network settings), so every vendor fact below comes from **search-result extracts of their pages**. That is weaker than reading the page. Confirm every size, price and stock level by email or phone before relying on it. **Inference** means our reading of the evidence, not something a vendor said.

## Short answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can we rent a full 10-hole course in Singapore? | **No.** Nobody in Singapore or Malaysia rents a full themed course. This confirms the 16 Nov finding in `04-sourcing.md`. |
| What *can* we rent? | **Single carnival-game putting lanes**, about 0.5–0.8 m wide and 1.5–3.6 m long, at about **S$100–550 each per day**, before GST, with delivery extra. |
| How good are they? | Good enough for a carnival game, but **a long way from our references.** Each is a short, flat lane with one cup: roughly a quarter of the area of one of our holes, with no props, no theme and no lighting. |
| How customisable? | **Branding only**: printed panels, stickers, maybe a colour scheme. You can't change the layout or obstacles. You can usually put your own free-standing props and lights *around* them. |
| Is there a way to rent something custom? | **Yes, but you rent from a builder, not a rental shop.** Ask set builders to also quote on a **hire basis**: they build, install and remove the course, and keep the materials afterwards. Since we're not planning reuse, owning the course gains us nothing. |

## Quality: what a rental lane really is (inference from the listings)

- **Shape:** one straight or zig-zag lane, 0.5–0.8 m wide and 1.5–3.6 m long, with one cup and a low border. One listing gives the height as about **10 cm**.
- **Compared with our holes:** our spec holes are 5–6 m long and 1.2–3.0 m wide, with props. One rental lane is about **1.5–2.5 m²**; one of ours is about **7 m²**.
- **Play:** a putting game, not a course. Expect 1–3 strokes and well under a minute per player. That's fine for children and for a queue, but adults will find it plain.
- **Look:** standard green turf and stock colours. Nothing glows. In a dark "Neon Countdown" room they'd need lighting from outside.
- **Condition:** unknown. These units go out to a lot of events, so turf wear and chipped edges are likely. **Ask for photos of the actual units you'll get**, not stock photos.
- **December demand:** Christmas parties and year-end dinners compete for the same stock, and event vendors add peak-season surcharges in Q4 ([eventspartner](https://eventspartner.com.sg/?p=58531)). Several golf items already show as out of stock.

## Customisation: what you can and can't change on a rental

| Change | Can we? | How |
|---|---|---|
| Printed branding, a number or a logo | **Usually yes** | The vendor prints a panel or sticker. Ask for the fee, the lead time, and a photo of a past branded job. |
| Colour scheme | **Sometimes** | Funco says so; others don't say |
| Layout, obstacles or length | **No** | The units are fixed. Pick from what they have. |
| Our own props next to or over a lane (number totem, small arch) | **Probably**, if they're free-standing | Get it in writing. Nothing taped, screwed or painted onto their units. |
| Lighting (LED, UV) | **Around them, yes** | Floor uplights and UV floods. Their turf probably won't react to UV. |
| Paint, screws or glue on their units | **No** | Damage-deposit risk |

## How we'd actually rent

1. **This week:** send template C in `06-rfq-templates.md` (updated 18 Nov with the questions below) to Propz, The Carnival Fair, Bigtop, Party People and CarnivalGuru. Funco is optional.
2. **In the same week:** send every set builder template A, which now asks for a **hire-basis price** too.
3. **Ask production** whether the church owns stage decks or risers that are free from 28 Dec to 1 Jan. If so, route 3 costs almost nothing.
4. **By Wed 25 Nov (G1):** if any rental is in the plan, put a hold on the date. Before paying a deposit, check:
   - delivery on 28–30 Dec, and whether hire days are counted while the units sit unused before the event;
   - collection on **Fri 1 Jan (a public holiday)** or on 2 Jan, and the surcharge for either;
   - deposit, damage and cancellation terms, and what counts as damage (turf wear, scuffs);
   - fire-rating paperwork for indoor use, if the venue needs it.
5. **On delivery:** photograph every unit, count putters and balls, and note any existing damage on the delivery order.

## Where rental fits our plan (recommendation, not a decision)

- **Don't rent the course.** It would play and look like a row of carnival games, not the references the team chose.
- **Do get one rental quote** as the price floor in `08-budget-model.md` and as the fallback at G1, as already planned.
- **Use 2–4 rented lanes, if any,** as extra kids' holes, a queue warm-up, or a backup if a built hole fails.
- **Ask builders for the hire-basis price.** That's the only way to "rent" a course that looks custom.
