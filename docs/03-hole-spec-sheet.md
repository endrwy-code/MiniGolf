# 10-hole spec sheet (draft v1)

**Status:** draft for the team to edit. Nothing here is decided. The dimensions are design targets, not measurements. This one document is meant for the builder, the turf supplier and the team.

**18 Nov (later):** the New York proposal in [`10-new-york-concept.md`](10-new-york-concept.md) would replace the ten holes below (names, props and some mechanics). The build standard below still applies to it. Nothing is decided until the team confirms.

**27 Nov:** for cups, follow `13-kulnari-benchmark.md` section 5.3: flush with the surface (1–3 mm below), sleeved, 60–68 mm deep, soft bottom. Any light ring or collar must be flush, never raised. Route C's details D1–D9 (`12-carpenter-pack.md`) are the floor-laid alternative to this deck standard, and iteration 1 (`14-new-york-iteration-1.md`) assumes them.

Play order runs **10 down to 1** (the countdown). Hole 1 is the finale, nearest the stage. Layout: `floorplan/layout-draft-zone-a.svg`.

## Build standard (applies to every hole)

| Item | Spec | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Base | 18 mm plywood deck on 45 mm timber battens (≤ 400 mm apart), on rubber levelling pads. Playing surface about **75 mm** above the floor (revised 17 Nov from 120 mm: one night and movable). | Enough depth for a sunk cup, less timber, lighter panels and a lower trip edge. Floor cables are routed around the course, not under it. Nothing gets screwed into the venue floor. |
| Modules | Each panel ≤ 2.44 × 1.22 m (one plywood sheet) and ≤ 35 kg. Bolted together with alignment dowels. Every panel labelled (e.g. `H7-B`). | Two people carry each panel, panels fit through doors, and set-up is fast and repeatable. |
| Level | ≤ 3 mm deviation over 2 m. Check with a level **and** a roll test with a ball. | A tilted lane is the most common reason a hole plays badly. |
| Rails (borders) | 100 mm above the playing surface, solid inner face, 45 mm thick. Cap rail about 70 mm wide with a **30–40 mm outside overhang** to hide the LED strip. Rounded corners. | Consistent rebound, a hidden LED glow and no sharp edges. |
| Playing surface | Short-pile putting turf (10–15 mm) or dense needle-punch carpet. Fix it with removable carpet tape or contact adhesive on the deck, not the floor. | Smooth, predictable roll. |
| Cup | 108 mm diameter (standard golf hole), about 60 mm deep (the ball is 43 mm), bottom about 10 mm above the floor, with a light ring or contrasting collar. | Fits the 75 mm deck and is readable in a dark room. |
| Tee | 300 × 300 mm rubber tee mat, or a painted circle, at the walkway end. | Clear start point. |
| Platform edges | Contrasting colour and LED glow on every exposed edge. A 1:4 bevel ramp at the tee entry. | Trip safety in low light. |
| Ball containment | 300 mm clear acrylic or mesh guard on any ramp or raised tier. | Keeps balls off the walkway and out of the crowd. |
| Hole totem | Printed board (about 600 × 900 mm) on a weighted base, about 1.6 m tall: big number, hole name, par. | Wayfinding and photos. |
| Electrics | **Low voltage only (12/24 V) on the course.** Power supplies sit off the course on a dedicated RCD-protected circuit, never ND18 or ND21. Battery power where possible. | Safety, and the existing overloaded circuits. |
| Moving parts | Only battery-powered (e.g. a 12 V geared motor), with the hole still playable if it stops. | No single point of failure. |
| Fire | Turf, paint, vinyl wraps and any foam need flame-retardant certificates to the venue's requirement. | Indoor public venue. |

**Turf to order:** about 72 m² net, plus about 15% for cutting waste and inlays, so **about 85 m²**. If the art direction uses two turf colours (base plus accent), split it about 70 / 15 m².

## Why this mix

- **4 hero holes (8, 4, 2, 1)** carry the look and the photos. Put the money and the best set builder there.
- **4 mid holes** add variety through mechanics (pins, tunnels, a ramp, a bridge) using cheap materials.
- **2 simple holes** at the start (10, 9) teach the turf speed and keep the queue moving. Players spend the least time there.
- The spend should go **where players linger and take photos**: the clock and the midnight finale.

## Things to decide before the design freeze (target: Mon 23 Nov)

1. Art direction: Bright City (recommended since 18 Nov, `09-city-concept.md`), Neon Countdown or Bright Carnival (see `02-reference-decoder.md`). This sets the turf colour. Bright City keeps every mechanic below and only changes the skins (e.g. windmill → Ferris wheel, barn → station).
2. Do the hole names and themes stay generic, or tie into the church's year, a scripture theme or the event theme? The team decides the content.
3. A moving windmill (battery motor) or static glow sails?
4. Which hole is the photo moment: the clock (2), the 2027 arch (1), or both?
5. Kids: add a shorter "junior tee" on each hole (cheap: one extra tee mat per hole)?
