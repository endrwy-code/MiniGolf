## 1. Before you start (10 minutes)

**Have ready:**

- The lane sheet `docs/17-lane-sizes-for-suppliers.png` on the computer or phone you'll use. Open it in the repo on GitHub and choose Download, or save it from the Claude chat.
- The team's **Taobao account** (it signs in to 1688), and the phone that receives that account's login codes. 1688 may ask to confirm the sign-in on that phone.
- A phone with **WhatsApp**. Use a team or work number if there is one: the supplier keeps the number and the profile name.
- A **shared team email address** if there is one, for the same reason.
- The name you'll sign with and the organisation name you'll give. Suppliers ask for a company name to put on a quote. Agree it within the team first.

**Don't send:**

- The carpenter's pack, floor plans, the 3D model or anything else from this repo except the lane sheet. They show the venue, which is Internal data. The lane sheet shows only sizes.
- The venue's name or address, or any member's personal details. Give a delivery address only when ordering.
- Any money. Pay only through 1688's or Tmall's checkout, or Alibaba Trade Assurance, and only once the team has decided.

**When to send:** China is on Singapore time (UTC+8). Sales staff work about 9 am to 6 pm, Monday to Saturday, and many 1688 sellers reply into the evening. A message sent this morning normally gets an answer today.

**Correction to the 22 Nov message.** The GL025 message in `14-china-factory-contacts.md` says "about 83 m² in total", but its ten lane sizes add up to **102.5 m²**: 83 m² is the shaped playing area inside the rectangles. A factory works from the rectangles, so the messages below give 102.5 m². If the decks are full rectangles, the GL025 estimate becomes about ¥27,700–30,800 (≈ S$5.5–6.2k) before freight, about 1.1 t and about 10 m³ (scaled from the 22 Nov figures; not quotes).

## 3. 1688 chat (A2 today; A10, A11, A6 later)

Use a computer if you can: pasting Chinese and attaching the image is easier. The phone steps follow.

**On a computer:**

1. Open Chrome and go to the shop's address, for example `ybgolf8.1688.com` for Yibang. From Singapore, 1688 may open in its English overseas view ("1688 AliTrading", with *SG-English-SGD* at the top), as it did for the browsing agent. Either view works.
2. **Sign in.** Click the person icon at the top right (or 登录, "log in"). Choose to sign in with Taobao (淘宝账号登录). The quickest way is to scan the QR code with the Taobao app: open Taobao on the phone, tap the scan icon at the top of the home screen, scan the code, then confirm on the phone. If 1688 asks to verify by SMS, the code goes to the phone on the Taobao account.
3. **Check it's the right shop.** In the shop's menu bar, click **Contact information** (联系方式). Check the company name, **佛山市顺德区异邦高尔夫用品有限公司** (*Foshan shunde yibang golf supplies co., ltd*), the **超级工厂** badge, and an address in Beijiao town (北滘镇梁场) at the foot of the page. The phone, mobile and contact name may be hidden (暂无, "none", and just 女士 or 先生), as the team saw on 24 Nov; that's fine, because you'll use the chat. If the company name is different, stop and check.
4. **Open the chat.** Click the blue **在线沟通** ("chat online") button next to the contact's name, or **Customer service** at the top right of the shop banner. A chat window opens. If 1688 offers to install its desktop chat app (阿里旺旺 / 1688 工作台), you can install it, or close the prompt and use the phone app below instead.
5. **Check who you're chatting with.** The chat header should show **zhoutao8004145**, and the panel on the right should name 佛山市顺德区异邦高尔夫用品有限公司 with 经营模式 生产厂家 (business type: manufacturer). Ignore the **开启智能找品询盘** (AI inquiry) button: it sends your request to other suppliers too.
6. **Paste the message in two parts.** The chat box takes at most **500 characters** per message (the counter shows *0 / 500*), so Message 1 (Chinese) in section 8 is split into Part 1 (about 330 characters) and Part 2 (about 390). Replace the two square-bracket placeholders in Part 1 with your organisation and name, paste Part 1 into the chat box and press **Enter** or **send**. Then paste Part 2 and send it. Pasting doesn't send by itself; pressing Enter does.
7. **Send the lane sheet.** Click the picture icon (the second icon above the text box), choose `17-lane-sizes-for-suppliers.png` and send it.
8. **Wait for the reply.** An automatic greeting may arrive straight away. A person usually answers within an hour in working hours. Replies appear in the chat window, under the message icon at the top right of 1688, and in the 1688 phone app.
9. **Read the reply.** In Chrome, right-click the page and choose *Translate to English*, or paste the reply into a translator.

**On a phone (the 1688 app):**

1. Install **阿里巴巴1688** from the App Store or Google Play (search "1688").
2. Open it, choose to log in with Taobao (淘宝登录) and approve the login in the Taobao app.
3. Tap the search bar and paste the company name, for example 佛山市顺德区异邦高尔夫用品有限公司 for Yibang, or paste the shop's link. Open the shop (it should carry the 超级工厂, "super factory", badge).
4. On the shop or any of its product pages, tap **客服** or **旺旺** (customer service) at the bottom left. The chat opens.
5. Paste Message 1 (Chinese) and send. Then tap **+** → **相册** (photo album) → choose the lane sheet → **发送** (send).
6. Allow notifications for the 1688 app so replies aren't missed.

**If the seller asks to move to WeChat** (可以加微信吗? "Can we add each other on WeChat?"): that's normal in China. Their WeChat is usually the mobile number on the contact page. Say yes (section 6), but **repeat the price, the dates and anything agreed in the 1688 chat**: if something goes wrong, 1688's dispute team looks only at the 1688 chat.

## 6. WeChat (only if a seller asks, or WhatsApp fails)

1. Install **WeChat** from the App Store or Google Play and tap **Sign Up**, with a Singapore mobile number. Enter the SMS code.

5. Paste the message (Chinese for Mr Zhou; English is fine for Ryan Wu) and send. Then tap **+** → **Album**, pick the lane sheet, tick **Original** (原图) so it stays sharp, and send.

## 9. After you send

1. **Log it.** In `docs/14-china-factory-contacts.csv`, change the supplier's `status` from "not contacted" to, for example, "messaged 24 Nov (1688 chat)", and later "quote received 25 Nov". Or send the replies (copied text or screenshots) back to Claude to log, in the CSV and in `04-sourcing.md`. Cover the team's account name and phone number in any screenshot before it goes into the repo.
2. **Chase once.** No reply by 5 pm the next working day: send Message 4. Still nothing by Thu 26 Nov: treat that channel as dead for this event.
3. **Check every quote** against "What to get in writing before paying" in `14-china-factory-contacts.md`: real photos or video, a delivery date in Singapore no later than 10 Dec, the fire-test report, an itemised price, and the same company name on the licence, the quote and the bank account.
4. **Expect the sizes to change.** If the tiles come on a 60 cm grid, the factory may suggest lanes such as 6.0 × 1.8 instead of 6.0 × 1.6. Don't accept on the spot: send the suggested sizes back to Claude so the layout and its clearances can be re-checked (`tools/make_layout_nyc.py`) before anyone confirms.
5. **Get the landed price**: goods + freight (Message 3) + 9% GST. Then decide by Fri 27 Nov, as in `14-china-factory-contacts.md`: GL025 decks plus local rails, or route C's local surface.
