## Huadong Play: the full assessment

**Verdict: credible manufacturer, wrong product for this job.** Huadong is a real playground exporter that has been shipping for a long time. Its mini golf is a new *outdoor landscape* line shown only in renders. We found no installed mini golf case, no indoor or neon design and no price. A custom build is very unlikely to reach Singapore by about 21 Dec.

Evidence: `reference/supplier-evidence/huadong-mini-golf-product-images.jpg` (all 12 images from their mini golf product pages).

### Red flags
- **No installed mini golf anywhere**, and the product imagery is all renders.
- The spec sheet looks **copied from their playground template**. It mentions a "stepped ladder" and PVC coating "to prevent bruising". It cites ASTM F1487 (a playground standard), ASTM E1918 (solar reflectance) and OHSAS 18001 (replaced in 2021). None of these is a mini golf credential.
- Their holes are outdoor, kerbed and much bigger than an auditorium can take. **Nothing indoor, modular, LED or neon is shown.**
- Inconsistent claims: the website says 40+ countries, the catalogue says 120+.
- 100% of the payment is made before goods leave China.
- We couldn't check their Alibaba status: the page returned a captcha. A "19 yrs" badge appears in search snippets but is unconfirmed.

### Green flags
- They've been exporting continuously since at least 2006, and still were this month.
- IAAPA-listed manufacturer with trade-show presence.
- They've built at least one mini golf exhibit.
- Outdoor-grade materials would suit a *permanent* outdoor course.

### Could they make 31 Dec?
Sea freight from Ningbo to Singapore takes about 5–14 days, plus 3–5 days of booking at origin and 2–4 days of customs and delivery. For arrival around 21 Dec, goods must leave the factory by about **4–10 Dec**. That needs design approval and a deposit by **20–23 Nov**, with production finished in about 12 working days. That's only plausible for near-catalogue outdoor holes, which aren't what we want. **A custom indoor design will not make it.**

### If you still want to talk to them (for 2027)
Use template D in `06-rfq-templates.md`, and make sure to ask:
1. Real photos or video of a mini golf course they've *installed*, ideally indoors; exactly what each hole includes; and whether it stands freely on a hall floor.
2. Written dates for design approval, ex-factory and delivery to Singapore, with a refund if they're late; and payment through Alibaba Trade Assurance or another protected method.
3. An itemised FOB and DAP Singapore quote; their business licence, showing the contract name matches the bank account holder; and fire-retardancy test reports for indoor turf.
