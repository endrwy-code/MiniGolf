### What it means for our design (Claude's reading, not decided)

- **It's a deck, not a rail.** The 3 cm lip is rounded on top. It keeps a gentle putt on the green, but a firm putt can ride over it, and a bank shot off it won't be reliable. Our holes depend on bank shots, and the art direction depends on the ribbed rail with its white cap and LED, so **the 140 m of rail stays a local build either way**. The 20 Nov version of this note assumed a factory lane would replace the rail; this product doesn't.
- **What it does replace:** the playing surface and route C's ten raised greens. Every lane stands 5 cm off the floor on real putting turf, the cup can go anywhere, and the holes look closer to the full New York design's 75 mm decks than route C's floor-laid carpet does.
- **The rail has to fit around it.** A 95 mm timber rail standing on the floor would rise only a few centimetres above the tiles. Ask the factory for straight edge pieces without the lip; then either fix the rail on the tiles or use taller timber (about 145 mm). Settle it at the prototype.
- **Cost.** Inside route C, the surface line is S$650 for carpet grass, or about S$2.1k with putting turf (`08-budget-model.md` §6). The GL025 is ≈ S$4.5–5k plus freight and 9% GST, and saves some plywood and about a carpenter day on the raised greens. So it adds very roughly S$3–4k before freight. In the full New York design it replaces about half the plywood (the decks), the battens and the turf (≈ S$2.6–5.2k of materials in `08-budget-model.md` §8) plus the deck carpentry, so there it's roughly cost-neutral on materials and saves labour.
- **Freight is the big unknown, for cost and for time.** About 8 m³ counts as roughly 1.3 t by air (airlines charge by volume when it's bulky), so air freight could come close to the price of the goods. Sea freight (LCL) from Foshan is far cheaper, and standard parts leave the factory within 48 hours, but door to door usually takes about two weeks or more. That works only with an order by about 25 Nov, and it misses 10 Dec if anything slips. Ask a forwarder to quote both. (These are estimates, not quotes.)
- **There's only one supplier.** All four PGM storefronts are the same company, so two quotes from them don't compete. The fallback is route C's local surface, which needs nothing from China.
- **The fire rating is unknown.** Ask for the test report. The venue's requirement is still an open question.

## Finding the factory behind a Taobao or Pinduoduo shop (do it on a phone)

**Search terms** (paste into Taobao, Pinduoduo or the 1688 app):

| Chinese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 迷你高尔夫球道 | mini golf lane |
| 移动迷你高尔夫 / 便携式迷你高尔夫 | mobile / portable mini golf |
| 高尔夫推杆果岭 定制 | putting green, made to order |
| 模块化 推杆果岭 拼接 | modular putting green, joins together |
| 室内迷你高尔夫 球场 定制 | indoor mini golf course, made to order |
| 迷你高尔夫 障碍 道具 | mini golf obstacles and props |
| 果岭草 人造草 定制尺寸 | putting turf, cut to size |
| 夜光 / 荧光 迷你高尔夫 | glow / UV mini golf |

**Then, for each promising shop:**

1. Look for 工厂店 or 源头工厂 (factory shop), a shop that's several years old and has real buyer photos (买家秀) of the product at full size.
2. Open the shop's licence: on Taobao, the shop page → 店铺资质 / 营业执照; on Pinduoduo, the shop page → 店铺资质. Note the company name (公司名称).
3. Search that company name on the **1688** app (the wholesale side of Taobao): many Taobao factory shops sell there too, with a 定制 (custom) option and a phone number on the company page (联系方式).
4. Check it on 企查查, 天眼查 or 爱企查: registered address, phone, founding date and whether it's a manufacturer (生产) or a trader (贸易). Prefer manufacturers in Guangdong (Shenzhen, Foshan, Huizhou, Dongguan), where most of these products come from.
5. Message the shop's customer service (客服): 你们是工厂吗？可以按我们的尺寸定做吗？可以加微信吗？ ("Are you the factory? Can you make it to our sizes? Can we add you on WeChat?"), then send the Chinese message above.

**Small stock items** (cups, flags, putters, balls, practice mats) can be bought straight from Taobao and shipped through Taobao's own consolidated shipping to Singapore (官方集运). Check the current transit time in the app before relying on it for anything on the critical path.
