# Mini golf lane sourcing: summary

*Research done 21–22 Nov 2026 on 1688 (signed in), Taobao/Tmall (signed in, in your Chrome) and Alibaba.com. 22 rows in `sourcing-results.csv`, screenshots in `screenshots/`. No sellers were contacted.*

## Shortlist (ranked)

1. **PGM GL025 modular green (拼接式果岭), from the PGM factory group. Rows 18 (Tmall) and 22 (1688), score 9.** This is the best fit found, and it came from the Taobao search.
   - **Construction:** a base of interlocking 60 × 60 cm PP plastic tiles, 5 cm high, with a **3 cm raised edge**, under two-tone 16 mm putting turf. One person can assemble it, and it packs into 60 × 60 cm boxes. That solves the 2.4 m piece limit.
   - **Price:** custom sizes on Tmall are **¥270–300/m²**, so 83 m² comes to about ¥22,400–24,900 (about S$4,500–5,000) before freight. The 1688 factory store lists standard sizes at about ¥220–300/m² with 48-hour dispatch.
   - **Track record:** 68 sold on Tmall. A buyer says it looks good in a venue and is easy to take apart.
   - **Factory contact:** Foshan Shunde Yibang Golf Supplies Co., the 1688 factory store (超级工厂). Mr Zhou Tao, 0757-2639 9246 or 130 7741 4019, at the same Beijiao Liangchang site as Foshan Qingzhou.
   - **Watch-outs:** the edge is 3 cm, lower than the 50–100 mm wanted. A 2 × 4 m green ships as 87.4 kg gross (about 11 kg/m²), so **83 m² is roughly 900 kg**, which makes air freight expensive.
2. **Foshan Qingzhou Artificial Grass Co. (PGM), rows 01–02, score 8.** This is the same group's export arm on Alibaba: TÜV-checked factory, 311 staff. Its GL006 and GL025 greens are made to your drawing at US$40/m² (10–99 m²) or US$22.88/m² (100 m² and up). Contacts: Ryan Wu, +86 181 2358 8327, ryan@mypgmgolf.com. It's the easiest route for export paperwork and a quote in US$. Ask Ryan specifically for the modular GL025.
3. **Jiangmen Qingzhou Sporting Goods Co., rows 05 and 17, score 8 (row 17 provisional).** A sister factory in the same group, selling the GL025 and a GL023 custom course at US$31.43/m². Its average dispatch is 17 days.
4. **Huizhou Linghang Sports Development Co., row 13, score 5.** Any size or shape made to your drawing, with 7-day dispatch. About S$74 (¥370) per 1×3 m green, minimum 10 pieces. Contact: Ms Li, 175 3998 1203.
5. **Zhongshan Cartoff Sports (PGK brand), row 14, score 5.** Any size at about S$35.65 (¥179) per m², non-slip backing, 48-hour shipping. Contact: Mr Xu, 135 6063 6234.
6. **Huizhou Shenger Sports Goods Co., row 06, score 5.** Custom greens for event rental on an EVA foam base, but slow (19–20 days), and its factory claim doesn't match a 2021 company in an office unit.

**Price benchmarks:** Guangzhou Chuzhuang sells LED glow lanes at US$437 per 0.61 × 3.81 m lane (row 10). Guangzhou Infinity sells a complete neon course for US$10,400–11,990 (row 12).

## What the Taobao search added

- **The PGM Tmall flagship (row 18) is the only listing with joining pieces *and* a raised edge**, and it led straight to the factory store on 1688 (row 22).
- **The other Taobao shops add little.** Rows 19–21 are a Shenzhen seller of shaped 9-hole greens (also sold under another shop's name), a Hangzhou retailer of large flat greens, and a Beijing seller whose images look AI-generated.
- **Most Taobao "mini golf" results are small home putting mats**, which the brief says to skip.
- **The PGM group** turns out to be one site in Beijiao, Shunde, trading as Foshan Shunde Yibang (1688 factory store), Foshan Qingzhou (Alibaba/1688), Jiangmen Qingzhou (sister factory) and Guangzhou Qingzhou (sales office). They all share the same phone and fax numbers.

## What surprised me

- **Ordering 100 m² from PGM on Alibaba is cheaper than ordering 83 m²** (US$2,288 vs about US$3,320), though that quote is for the GL006 roll-out green, not the modular GL025.
- **Nobody mentions a fire rating** (阻燃 / B1) on any listing.
- **Timing and freight are the main risks.** Custom work averages 11–20 days to dispatch, while standard sizes ship in 13–48 hours. Building the lanes from standard modular sizes plus loose tiles may be the realistic way to make 10 December. For the modular product, ask for an air-freight quote on about 900 kg early.

## What I couldn't access or verify

- **Taobao blocked the detail sections.** After a few product pages, Taobao showed a 访问异常 ("abnormal access") notice and hid the description sections of further product pages. I stopped there rather than try to get around it. For the same reason, **no Taobao shop licences were opened**, so the companies behind rows 19–21 are unverified. The PGM product was traced through its brand to the 1688 factory instead.
- **Not done:** Pinduoduo, company-register checks (企查查 / 天眼查) and Made-in-China.
- **Alibaba keyword search** hit a slider captcha. Lead B3 (Guangzhou Shuangfa) is unverified.
- **Prices on 1688 pages display in S$** (1688 converts automatically). The yuan figures in brackets are from search results.
