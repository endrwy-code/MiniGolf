## Answer in one paragraph

The best match is the **PGM GL025 modular putting green (拼接式果岭)**, made by the PGM group in Beijiao, Shunde, Foshan. It's built from 60 × 60 cm interlocking PP base tiles, 5 cm high, with a 3 cm raised edge and 16 mm two-tone putting turf. It comes in custom sizes and shapes and packs into 60 cm boxes, so it meets the 2.4 m piece limit and lies flat on a smooth floor. Tmall price for custom work is ¥270–300/m², about ¥22,400–24,900 for 83 m². Its 1688 factory store ships standard sizes within 48 hours. Main risks: the edge is lower than the 50–100 mm wanted; the set weighs about 11 kg/m² (about 900 kg for 83 m²), so air freight will be costly; and no fire rating is mentioned anywhere.

## Gaps and changes from the brief

- **Taobao:** after a few product pages, Taobao showed a 访问异常 ("abnormal access") notice and hid product descriptions. I stopped rather than work around it. As a result, **no Taobao shop licences were opened**, and rows 19–21 have unverified companies. The PGM product was traced through its brand to the 1688 factory instead.
- **Not done:** Pinduoduo, Made-in-China, and company-register checks (企查查, 天眼查, 爱企查).
- **Alibaba.com keyword search** always hit a slider captcha, so I used supplier storefronts and Alibaba's category pages instead.
- **Factory status is unverified** for rows 06, 07, 13, 14 and 19–21.
- **No listing states a fire rating** (阻燃 or B1).
- **Prices on 1688 pages display in S$** (1688 converts automatically). Yuan figures come from search results.
